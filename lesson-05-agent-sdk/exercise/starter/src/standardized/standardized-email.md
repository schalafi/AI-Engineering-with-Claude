### Risk Assessment

**🚨 CRITICAL ISSUES - High Risk Contract:**

1. **Missing Party Information:**
   - Customer name/entity not formally identified
   - No legal entity name for DesignCo
   - No authorized signatory designation

2. **Undefined Scope:**
   - "Website redesign services" is extremely vague
   - No deliverables specified
   - No acceptance criteria defined
   - No revision/change request process

3. **Missing Legal Protections:**
   - No liability limitations or caps
   - No indemnification clauses
   - No warranty disclaimers
   - No force majeure provisions
   - No dispute resolution mechanism
   - No governing law specified

4. **IP Rights Undefined:**
   - No intellectual property ownership terms
   - No license grants specified
   - Unclear who owns final deliverables
   - No third-party IP provisions

5. **Incomplete Payment Terms:**
   - "Completion" not defined - what constitutes completion?
   - No late payment penalties
   - No refund policy
   - No payment method specified

6. **Termination Gaps:**
   - No termination rights defined
   - No notice period requirements
   - No provisions for work-in-progress payment
   - No post-termination obligations

7. **Data & Confidentiality:**
   - No confidentiality obligations
   - No data protection/privacy terms
   - No security requirements

8. **Formality Issues:**
   - Informal email format (not a binding contract)
   - No signature blocks
   - No execution date
   - Lacks legal contract structure

**RECOMMENDATION:** This informal proposal should NOT be treated as a binding contract. A formal written agreement should be drafted addressing all missing terms before any work begins or deposits are paid.
